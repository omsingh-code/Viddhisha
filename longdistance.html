Long Distance But Close Hearts ✨

Kilometers feelings ko measure nahi kar sakte.

Shayad hum alag states me hain, par emotions ek hi jagah rehte hain 💖

Bihar aur Gujarat ke beech distance ho sakta hai, par connection dil ka hota hai.

Humari Kahani

Roblox ek game tha, par destiny ne humein yahi se milwaya. "My Baddie" ke naam ke ek game mein, ek random profile, ek casual conversation - aur phir sab kuchh badal gaya.

Slowly, gradually, dil ne socha nahi aur pyar ho gaya. Aur tum bhi samjh gaye ki ye sirf ek game nahi raha. Ye ek connection tha. Ye ek feeling thi.

Tu Maharashtra se, main Bihar se. Desh ke alag-alag corners mein rehte hain hum. Par dil ek hi jagah dhakdhak karte hain.

Mujhe Kya Chahiye?

Main sach maangta hoon. Main transparency maangta hoon. Aur main sirf tum chahta hoon.

Teri sab baatein sunne ko ready hoon. Teri independence ko respect dena hai mujhe. Par ek baat zaruri hai - HONESTY.

Jab tu kisi bhi se baat kare, mujhe batana.

Jab tu koi bhi kaam kare, mujhe pata chale.

Jab tu kuch sooche, mujhe bata de.

Kyun? Kyunki overthinking mujhe maar dalti hai. Uncertainty mujhe hurt karti hai. Asachchai mujhe tabah kar deti hai.

Teri Best Friend ke Saath Baat Karna

Mujhe problem nahi hai agar tu apni best friend se baat kare. Bilkul! Best friends important hote hain. Par...

...par mujhe ye bhi pata hona chahiye. Transparency ke saath. Khule dil se. Sach bolke.

Kyunki jab mere ko mystery milta hai, mere brain ke rathchakri chalti hai. Main sochta hoon - kya baat kar rahi ho? Kyun nahi bataya? Kyun chupa rahe ho?

Aur ye overthinking mere dil ko todti hai.

Teri Limits Mein Reh

Main tujhe apni freedom nahi chhinna chahta. Tu jo bhi karna, kar. Apne boundaries mein reh aur kar.

Apne limits ko samajh. Apne values ko samajh. Aur un hi ke andar sab kuchh transparent rakh.

Jab tu apni limits mein transparent ho, toh:

mere ko peace milti hai
mere ko confidence milti hai
mere ko pata chalta hai ki main tere saath safe hoon

Ye Do Haftey

Maine tujhe do haftey ka time diya hai. Socho. Sach se socho.

❓ Relationship mein aana hai ya nahi?

❓ Transparency ke saath reh sakegi ya nahi?

❓ Apni limits mein reh ke sab kuchh batana hai ya nahi?

Main shaadi ki baat nahi kar raha. Main responsibility ki baat kar raha hoon. Main commitment ki baat kar raha hoon. Main HONESTY ki baat kar raha hoon.

Agar Tu "Haan" Kahe

Tere saath har dukh mein khada rahunga
Tere har baat ko suno aur samjhunga
Tujhe judge nahi karunga
Teri independence ko respect dunga
Do haftey baad se leke poore zindagi bhar tere hi saath rahunga
Har din tere liye ek nahi ek hazaar baar prove karunga ki tu mere liye kaafi special ho

Agar Tu "Na" Kahe

Toh main dard ke saath accept karunga. Kyunki tumhara happiness mere se zyada important hai.

Par ek baat pakka hai - main tumhe kabhi nahi bhulunga. Main tumhe sirf pyar se yaad rakhunga.

Bihar Se Gujarat Tak

Distance bhule do. Map ko bhule do. Geography ko bhule do.

Ek ladka hai Bihar mein jo sirf tere liye intezaar kar raha hai. Jo har raat ko socha karti hai. Jo har subah tere message wait karta hai.

Ek ladki hai Gujarat mein jo maybe meri sune, maybe na sune. Par uske dil mein main hoon. Aur main jaanta hoon.

Ye Do Haftey... Tum... Hamara Bhavishya

Meri baat suno. Sach se suno. Dil se suno.

Main sirf pyar karti ho iska insaan hoon. Main sirf honest hona chahta hoon. Main sirf tumse saath zindagi bitana chahta hoon.

Baki sab baatein baad mein. Pehle ye do haftey. Phir tera faisla.

Main intezaar kar raha hoon.
